the louisian community is mandated to exercise caution and ethical judgment in using social media and digital platforms. these platforms encompass university websites slu student portals official email systems and public online forums. posting libelous remarks unverified institutional rumors harassment or violating republic act 10173 data privacy act of 2012 is subject to administrative sanctions under student conduct policies.
the university clinic at the bonifacio and maryheights campuses provides emergency first aid outpatient medical consultations and routine dental checks. annual medical exams and physical fitness certificates are mandatory requirements for students taking physical education pathfit courses and laboratory practicums.
in case of natural calamities or seismic events in baguio city students must execute duck cover and hold and follow floor wardens toward designated open evacuation areas slu main oval and open courts. immediate security concerns should be reported to the security office located at gate 1 and gate 2.
minimum grade requirements saint louis university operates under a standard numerical grading system from 75 to 99 percent. a grade of 75 is the minimum passing mark for any academic subject. grades below 75 indicate failure. for specialized degree programs under the school of accountancy management computing and information studies samcis and school of engineering retention grades may be set higher by the academic department.
failure due to absences fda regular class attendance is required of all students. a student who incurs unexcused absences of more than 20 percent of the total class hours designated for a course during a term shall be automatically dropped from the course roll and given a grade of fda failure due to absences.
examination permits students are required to secure an official examination permit and present their validated slu rfid card prior to taking prelim midterm and final examinations. proctors reserve the right to bar any student from examination rooms without valid credentials.
academic integrity plagiarism unauthorized collaboration possession of unauthorized notes use of mobile devices during examinations or contract cheating constitutes major academic dishonesty subject to suspension or dismissal under the slu disciplinary board.
prescribed uniform and dress code students must strictly adhere to the university uniform code from mondays to thursdays. modest civilian attire is permissible only during designated wash days fridays and saturdays. slippers sandos revealing attire and apparel with obscene language are strictly forbidden within all slu campus locations.
what are the admission requirements for transferee students at slu?
slu admits transferees in all degree programs except bachelor of science in medical laboratory science mls subject to compliance with pertinent guidelines. transferees must undergo a qualifying examination qe personality evaluation and dean interview. required documents include an official transcript of records tor for evaluation certificate of transfer credential honorable dismissal and psa birth certificate.
how does a student request official academic transcripts and certifications?
requests for official transcripts of records tor certificates of grades cog and certificates of graduation must be filed online through the uro document request portal. clearance from the accounting department library services and dean of student affairs must be completed before physical or electronic release.
what is the enrollment procedure for continuing undergraduate students?
continuing students must complete online advising of subjects through the slu student portal settle the required downpayment through authorized payment partner banks or university cashiers and finalize schedule confirmation within their assigned year level window.
what is the procedure for dropping or adding subjects during the adjustment period?
adding or dropping of subjects is permitted only during the first two weeks of regular semesters upon submission of an accomplished adjustment form endorsed by the academic department chair and approved by the university registrar.
