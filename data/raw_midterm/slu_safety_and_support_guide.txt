1. SLU Guidelines on Social Media and Digital Platforms
The Louisian community is mandated to exercise caution and ethical judgment in using social media and digital platforms. These platforms encompass university websites, SLU Student Portals, official email systems, and public online forums. Posting libelous remarks, unverified institutional rumors, harassment, or violating Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) is subject to administrative sanctions under student conduct policies.


2. Center for Health Services and Clinic Protocols
The University Clinic at the Bonifacio and Maryheights campuses provides emergency first-aid, outpatient medical consultations, and routine dental checks. Annual medical exams and physical fitness certificates are mandatory requirements for students taking physical education (PATHFIT) courses and laboratory practicums.


3. Emergency and Evacuation Protocols
In case of natural calamities or seismic events in Baguio City, students must execute Duck, Cover, and Hold and follow floor wardens toward designated open evacuation areas (SLU Main Oval and open courts). Immediate security concerns should be reported to the Security Office located at Gate 1 and Gate 2.
