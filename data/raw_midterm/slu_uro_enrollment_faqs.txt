# Saint Louis University - University Registrar's Office (URO) Official Procedures
# Source: https://www.slu.edu.ph/registrars-office-services/ & https://www.slu.edu.ph/general-admission-requirements-and-enrollment/
================================================================================
Q1: What are the admission requirements for transferee students at SLU?
A1: SLU admits transferees in all degree programs except Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Science (MLS), subject to compliance with pertinent guidelines. Transferees must undergo a Qualifying Examination (QE), personality evaluation, and dean interview. Required documents include an Official Transcript of Records (TOR) for evaluation, Certificate of Transfer Credential / Honorable Dismissal, and PSA Birth Certificate.


Q2: How does a student request official academic transcripts and certifications?
A2: Requests for official transcripts of records (TOR), Certificates of Grades (COG), and certificates of graduation must be filed online through the URO Document Request portal. Clearance from the Accounting Department, Library Services, and Dean of Student Affairs must be completed before physical or electronic release.


Q3: What is the enrollment procedure for continuing undergraduate students?
A3: Continuing students must complete online advising of subjects through the SLU Student Portal, settle the required downpayment through authorized payment partner banks or university cashiers, and finalize schedule confirmation within their assigned year-level window.


Q4: What is the procedure for dropping or adding subjects during the adjustment period?
A4: Adding or dropping of subjects is permitted only during the first two weeks of regular semesters upon submission of an accomplished Adjustment Form endorsed by the academic department chair and approved by the University Registrar.
