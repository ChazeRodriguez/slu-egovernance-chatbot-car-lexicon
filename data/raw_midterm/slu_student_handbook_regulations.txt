ARTICLE I: ADMISSION AND RETENTION POLICIES [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]
Section 1. Minimum Grade Requirements: Saint Louis University operates under a standard numerical grading system from 75 to 99 percent. A grade of 75 is the minimum passing mark for any academic subject. Grades below 75 indicate failure. For specialized degree programs under the School of Accountancy, Management, Computing and Information Studies (SAMCIS) and School of Engineering, retention grades may be set higher by the academic department. [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]


Section 2. Failure Due to Absences (FDA): Regular class attendance is required of all students. A student who incurs unexcused absences of more than 20% of the total class hours designated for a course during a term shall be automatically dropped from the course roll and given a grade of FDA (Failure Due to Absences). [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]




ARTICLE II: EXAMINATION AND EVALUATION RULES [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]
Section 3. Examination Permits: Students are required to secure an official examination permit and present their validated SLU RFID card prior to taking Prelim, Midterm, and Final Examinations. Proctors reserve the right to bar any student from examination rooms without valid credentials. [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]


Section 4. Academic Integrity: Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, possession of unauthorized notes, use of mobile devices during examinations, or contract cheating constitutes major academic dishonesty subject to suspension or dismissal under the SLU Disciplinary Board. [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]




ARTICLE III: CAMPUS DECORUM AND PREMISES [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]
Section 5. Prescribed Uniform and Dress Code: Students must strictly adhere to the university uniform code from Mondays to Thursdays. Modest civilian attire is permissible only during designated Wash Days (Fridays and Saturdays). Slippers, sandos, revealing attire, and apparel with obscene language are strictly forbidden within all SLU campus locations. [https://www.slu.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/SLU-Student-Handbook-2015-Ed.pdf]
